# Planning Mods

4 hosted mods. Each entry opens its local mod page, source files and setup instructions.

- **[Goal Meter](goal-meter/)** by Nate Herk - Track completed tasks, elapsed time and estimated time remaining for your active goal, and view goals from other conversations in one place.
- **[Plan Bar](plan-bar/)** by Gary - Follow several plans at once through progress bars showing stages, completion percentages and waiting or failed states above the prompt.
- **[Plan Progress](plan-progress/)** by Kirill Serditov - Follow a multi-step plan through live progress bars above the prompt, with stage and step tracking plus optional cues for decisions, errors and completion.
- **[Task Recap](task-recap/)** by Hamza Zafar - Keep the current goal, active work, pending decisions and next step visible above the prompt, with a longer recap available on demand.
