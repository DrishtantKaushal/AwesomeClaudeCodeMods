# My trading rules

Written before I enter, checked against open positions. One rule per line, with a number that can be broken. Delete these examples and write your own.

- Max size per position: 5% of my trading bankroll.
- Take the cost basis out at 2x.
- Cut at -50% unless I have written down why I am holding.
- No new position within 24 hours of a loss over 20%.
- One thesis sentence before I buy, written in the journal block.
